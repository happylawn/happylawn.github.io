Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if the robot gets damaged?

Normal wear and tear is fully covered. If the robot stops working due to a technical fault, we repair or replace it at no extra cost. Intentional damage or theft is handled on a case-by-case basis.

What about my pets?

Modern robot mowers are incredibly safe. They feature collision sensors that cause them to stop and turn around if they bump into an object (or a pet), and lift sensors that instantly stop the blades if the mower is picked up.

Does the robot handle edges and borders?

The robot cuts right up to the perimeter wire. Depending on your landscaping, you may occasionally need to run a quick string trimmer around fences or retaining walls, but the bulk of your lawn is completely automated.
